# Incident triage workflow

Sentinel's synthetic anomalies are practice scenarios, not live alerts.

## Step 1: Establish scope
Record the displayed time window, all affected channels, and the severity shown in the anomaly registry. Expand the brush to check whether the behavior is isolated or sustained.

## Step 2: Test alternative explanations
Compare RPM with vibration and thermal changes. A speed transition, sensor artifact, or data-quality issue may explain an apparent fault.

## Step 3: Evaluate the brief
Run analysis and distinguish observed telemetry from hypotheses. Treat ranked hypotheses as leads, not diagnoses.

## Step 4: Escalate appropriately
For real systems, consult plant procedures and qualified personnel, preserve measurements, and follow existing safety protocols. Do not use this demo to issue machine-control instructions.
